What it is

  • A web app for singing practice. It runs in the browser of your phone or computer and uses your microphone. There is nothing to download.
  • You sing short melodies with a guide voice. The Gym draws your voice next to the melody, shows which notes landed and tells you what to work on.
  • It starts at your level. The free Vocal Analysis takes about 5 minutes. It places you in the right module and picks your key.
  • Three areas. Pitch Machines: pitch workouts, step by step. Technique Lab: how a sound feels as you move through the keys. The Mat: short exercises for your body before you sing.
  • English and Spanish.
